About CampusMeter

CampusMeter helps university students in Nigeria stretch an allowance from one payment to the next. It shows where your money is going, how long it can last, and what a safe daily limit looks like.

It is designed for students living on allowances from parents, guardians, scholarships or small businesses, and for anyone who has reached the last week with too little left.

This is the first version. It runs entirely in your browser with no account, and it gives estimates based on the numbers you enter. It is not financial advice or a guarantee.

  • Phone first. Designed for small Android screens and slow connections.
  • Private by design. Your budget is saved on your device in this version.
  • Free. No payment, no ads, no login.
